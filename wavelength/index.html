Wavelength

Read each other’s minds, one clue at a time.

How to play

You’re a team. Each round, one of you sees a scale like Cold ↔ Hot and a hidden spot on the dial, then gives a clue that points there. Your partner turns the dial to where they think the clue lands. A bullseye scores 4, close scores 3 or 2. Take turns giving clues for 8 rounds and see how in sync you are. You can play your turn whenever you like; the game waits for you.
