After Dark

Questions that get hotter every round. Grown-ups only.

How to play

Three levels, ten questions, one fire to keep going.
Read my mind: one of you answers about yourself and the other guesses what you picked. A match adds 3 🔥.
Open up: you both answer the same question, then reveal together. Nobody sees an answer until you’ve both sent yours.
After each reveal, tap 🔥 if the other person’s answer turned you on.
It starts flirty (Warming Up), gets explicit (Getting Hot), goes all the way (No Limits: kinks, fantasies, limits and safe words), and ends with what you want Tonight. Skip any question with “Different question.”
