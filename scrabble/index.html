Scrabble

Build words, cross the board, and make every tile count.

Use your usual Pounce name to keep your room seat. Words are checked against ENABLE.

How to play

Tap a tile and then a board square. Start across the center star. After that, connect to an existing word. All new words must read across or down.

Use all seven tiles for 50 extra points. Tap a staged tile to take it back, or use Recall. If you exchange tiles or pass, your turn ends.
