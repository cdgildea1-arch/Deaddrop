Drums

Rest the phone on your knee or foot. Stomp like you're on a kick pedal for the kick, and knock the top-left corner of the phone for the snare. The bottom strips play hi-hat (closed at the bottom, open at the top) and toms (floor tom at the bottom). Crash, open hat and floor tom stay on the screen.
