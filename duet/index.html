Codenames Duet

Two agents. One shared mission.

How to play

Give a one-word clue and a number to help your partner find the green agents on your key. Guessing a bystander ends the turn. Guessing an assassin ends the mission. Find all 15 agents together. With the timer gone, you can still win in sudden death by using earlier clues.
