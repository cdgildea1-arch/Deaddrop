How to play

Everyone plays at once. Tap a card in your hand, then a center pile one rank higher or lower. Suits don’t matter; A connects to 2 or K. Your hand refills automatically.

First to clear all 20 cards wins. If nobody can play, the center flips automatically. This is our party version of Speed: two players use two piles; three or four use four. Each player has their own shuffled deck.
