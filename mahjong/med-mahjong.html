A small hand, a clear goal

Tap a printed hand and choose dots, bamboo, or characters to make it your goal. The green-outlined tiles in your hand fit that goal. Start with 13 tiles. Draw one, then choose a tile to discard. The other person and the computer players take their turns. Complete one of the printed 14-tile hands and tap Call Mahjong.

Pick one numbered suit for a goal: blue dots, green bamboo, or red characters. A number and its color must match. Winds, flowers, and jokers are separate tiles. The suits cannot be mixed within a goal.

Joker house rule: A joker can replace one missing tile in a group of three or four, if that group still has a natural tile. It cannot replace a pair. There are no tile calls or exposures in this quick version. The printed goals are made for this game, not an official annual Mahjong card.

Friendly deal: Each real player starts close to a suggested printed hand, and their next draws include a path to finish it. You may choose another hand, but that can be harder. The computer players cannot win during the opening turns. Your discards still decide whether you complete a hand.

The room saves each move and message. You can leave and come back using the same code on this device. An internet connection, including cellular data, is needed to send or retrieve online turns.

Meet the tiles

Dots, bamboo, and characters each run from 1 to 9. E and W are winds, F is a flower, and J is a joker. There are four of each tile type in this little game.

The art is simplified so the tiles are easy to read on a phone.
