Hearts

Low score wins. Watch out for the queen of spades.

How to play

Pass three cards left, right, then across on successive hands. Keep your cards on every fourth hand. The 2♣ opens. Follow suit if you can. Lead hearts only after one has been played, unless you have only hearts.

Each heart costs one point and Q♠ costs 13. Take all 26 in a hand to give everyone else 26 instead. Once someone reaches 100, the lowest score wins. A tie for lowest means another hand.
