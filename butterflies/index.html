Butterflies

Questions that get a little closer every round.

How to play

Three levels, ten questions, one jar to fill.
Read my mind: one of you answers about yourself and the other guesses what you picked. A match adds 3 🦋.
Open up: you both answer the same question, then reveal your answers together.
After each reveal, tap 🦋 if the other person’s answer got you. That adds one more.
It starts light (First Flutter), gets warmer (Getting Closer), goes deep (Head Over Heels), and ends with a love note from each of you. Answer whenever you like; the game waits for you.
