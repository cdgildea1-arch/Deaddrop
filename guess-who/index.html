Guess Who?

Pick a face. Ask yes-or-no questions. Make your guess.

How to play

Each player secretly chooses a character. Take turns asking a yes-or-no question or making a final guess. Answers narrow your board automatically. Use Mark cards to cross out or restore your own notes. A correct guess wins. A wrong guess gives the round to your opponent.
