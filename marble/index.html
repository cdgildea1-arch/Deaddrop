How to play

Set your phone face-up on a table, then tip it to roll. A small tilt is a gentle roll and a steep one is a sprint. The marble keeps its momentum, so start braking before each turn.

Each course is a run of mazes. Your time keeps adding up from maze to maze, and the lowest total wins. If you fall in a hole you go back to the last flag, and the clock keeps running.

Holes sit in the outside corners of turns, so taking a corner too fast drops you in. Ramps show chevrons pointing downhill: rolling with them speeds you up, and rolling against them takes a hard tilt. Ice barely slows you down, and sand bogs you down.

Marbles handle differently. Steel is heavy, so ramps and sand bother it less, but it's slow to stop. Rubber grips and bounces. Cat's Eye is quick but slippery. Galaxy is light and gets pushed around by ramps.

Every course is the same maze every time, so you can learn it and get faster. The Daily changes each day. On a computer, use the arrow keys.
