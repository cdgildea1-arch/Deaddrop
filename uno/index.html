UNO!

Match the color. Change the direction. Race to zero.

How to play

Match by color, number, or symbol. Wilds let you choose a color. Draw Four requires no card of the active color. Draw cards skip the affected player and don’t stack. If your drawn card matches, play it or pass.

Call UNO with two cards before playing down to one. Miss it and draw two automatically. First out wins. With Party cards on, a Wild can swap hands with any player or make every opponent draw one.
